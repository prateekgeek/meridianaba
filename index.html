Our Mission

More Than Just a Provider. A Partner.

Meridian Behavioral Pathways was founded on a simple belief: Effective therapy shouldn't feel like a clinical appointment. It should feel like a natural part of your child's day.

Our therapists specialize in Natural Environment Teaching (NET). By using the toys your child already loves, we build meaningful communication and social skills in real-time.

First, we schedule a free consultation. If we are a clinical fit, we conduct a comprehensive assessment (VB-MAPP, ABLLS-R, or AFLS) to develop your child's individualized treatment plan.

We work with major insurance providers and offer private pay options. Our intake specialist will handle the benefit verification process for you.
